Digital Workplace Skills: Foundations

Module 1 • Digital Bridge Accessibility Curriculum

1. Introduction to Digital Tools

Modern distributed organizations rely on collaborative text editors, issue trackers, and direct messaging channels. Ensuring that communication remains accessible requires applying heading structures, alternative text for diagrams, and clear link text.

2. Key Communication Principles

  • Clarity: State purpose upfront in tickets and messages.
  • Alternative Text: Never post a screenshot without describing its essential content.
  • Asynchronous Coordination: Respect quiet hours and network constraints across regional teams.
